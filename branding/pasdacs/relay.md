# Applying to Relay

Relay is the PASDACS product, so it wears the PASDACS identity in full. The "Night & Signal" navy and teal palette from the Relay vision is replaced by this pack's colours.

## Name and lockup

- Full name **RELAY by PASDACS**: logo, page titles, documents. Use `relay-lockup.svg` on light grounds and `relay-lockup-dark.svg` on dark ones (Relay group).
- The lockup is the PASDACS mark with RELAY in Manrope 800 (tracked +3) and "BY PASDACS" in small caps under it, in `ink-muted`. The fast-forward chevrons now carry the hand-off idea: work moving forward between agents and people without being dropped.
- In sentences and labels write **Relay**. Inside the product, Relay is also the agent's name.
- Partner (white-label) workspaces show the partner's own logo, with `powered-by.svg` ("Powered by PASDACS Relay") in the footer, sign-in page, emails and PDFs.
- Module names: **D2D** (Data to Decision) only for the dashboard builder; **Ask Relay** for conversational analytics (formerly Ask D).
- D2D has its own mark in the same tile: a D cut into rows of data, the middle row in mint (D2D group). Use `d2d-lockup.svg` on light grounds and `d2d-lockup-dark.svg` on dark ones.

## In the product UI

- **Themes.** Light is the default, matching pasdacs.com. Dark uses the dark values of every semantic token: `primary-fill` flips to near-white, and mint text and fills use the bright `accent-500` with near-black `on-accent`.
- **Mint means proven.** Use `accent-text` / `success` for "passed", "proven", "approved" and live status. Don't use it for decoration in dense screens. Danger, waiting and other states get their own words and icons.
- **Agent vs person.** Show agent output in `mono` (`terminal` style) and decisions by people in `sans`. The Validation queue, review gates and evidence packs read best as `radius-card` panels joined by `line` hairlines.
- **Density.** The website spacing (`section-y-*`) is for marketing pages only. App screens use the same tokens at smaller steps: `card-pad` for panels, `btn-h-sm` / `btn-h-md` buttons.
- **Voice in the UI.** The same rules as the brand book, plus the Relay word list: Decide (Keep, Merge, Retire); Proven; Programme view; Sample estate; Rules first · AI-led · Rules only; Validation queue; Hand over.

## Not settled yet

- The proposed Relay lockup and the dark theme are new. Neither is on pasdacs.com yet.
- The D2D mark and lockups (D2D group) are new too. The D2D Relay app shows them from its next release (the rail, the sign-in page, the browser tab and app icons).
- The site has no Plus Jakarta Sans, navy or teal anywhere, so any Relay screen, deck or email that still uses them needs to change.
