Contact

PASDACS runs D2D Relay

Write to info@pasdacs.com about anything: help with D2D Relay, credits and payments, connecting your warehouse, Migration D2D, your data and privacy, or a complaint. Write from the address you sign in with, so we can find your organisation.

From inside D2D Relay

  • Request credits (the organisation's name in the top bar): asks PASDACS for credits.
  • Ask PASDACS to connect it (Connections): your own warehouse or database.
  • Contact PASDACS (Migration D2D): moving dashboards from one BI tool to another.

Each of these sends us what we need to answer, and we reply by email.

Privacy requests and complaints

We answer within 30 days and settle every grievance within 90 days, as the privacy notice says. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
