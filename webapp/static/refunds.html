Refund and cancellation policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Credits for D2D Relay are paid for in advance. Once a payment is made it is final: there are no refunds and no cancellations. Questions: info@pasdacs.com.

No refunds

Credits bought are not refundable, in whole or in part. That includes credits not used yet, credits left when an organisation stops using D2D Relay or is erased, and credits used for dashboards or answers you later do not want.

No cancellations

A purchase cannot be cancelled once the payment is made. Before you pay, the app shows the pack, its price and the GST.

Delivery

Credits are a digital service. They are added to your organisation's balance as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment, and the receipt or GST invoice is emailed and kept under Organisation > Payments. Nothing is shipped.

When a run does not finish

A dashboard build, a change or an Ask Relay question that fails, is stopped or is cut off gives its credits back on its own. This returns credits to your balance, not money.

Payment errors

If you were charged and no credits arrived, or charged twice for one purchase, write to info@pasdacs.com with the Razorpay payment reference within 30 days. We check it with Razorpay and put it right: by adding the missing credits, or by returning the duplicate payment. A payment that failed at your bank is returned by Razorpay or the bank under their own rules.
