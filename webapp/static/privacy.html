D2D Relay by PASDACS

Privacy notice

Effective 11 November 2026

PASDACS runs D2D Relay (D2D: Data to Decision, a Relay Platform product). This notice says which personal data D2D Relay holds, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and how you can see it, correct it or have it erased. Questions and grievances: info@pasdacs.com.

What we hold

  • You: your work email address and your name, your organisation and your role in it (owner or member).
  • Sign-ins: when you signed in, your IP address and your browser. Sign-in codes are stored only as a salted hash and expire after 10 minutes.
  • What you put in: the files you upload, reference images, what you describe, your feedback and your Ask Relay questions; and what D2D Relay makes from them: plans, data models, prototypes and Power BI files.
  • Warehouse connections, when PASDACS switches a connector on for your organisation: their details, with passwords and keys encrypted.
  • Usage: what was built or changed, the credits used and requested, and what you ask PASDACS for in the app (a connector, Migration D2D), with your message.
  • Payments, when your organisation buys credits online: the pack, the amount, when, who paid and Razorpay's payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details go straight to Razorpay; D2D Relay never sees them.
  • Billing details, for GST invoices: your organisation's legal name and address, its state or country, and its GSTIN if it gives one; and the invoices issued.

Cookies are used only to sign you in (for up to 12 hours at a time). There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who in your organisation sees what

  • Your organisation on D2D Relay is everyone who signs in with an address at its email domain: the first person from the domain creates it and owns it, and everyone after them joins it.
  • Everyone in it sees its people: their names, email addresses, roles and when they last signed in.
  • Its owners can open every dashboard in the organisation, with its files, and manage its people, credits, payments and billing details. Other people see their own dashboards and the ones shared with them.

Why

  • To sign you in and keep your organisation's work apart from everyone else's.
  • To build the dashboards you ask for and answer the questions you ask.
  • To run credits and payments for them, to send receipts, and to help you when you write to us.
  • For security: every sign-in is recorded, and PASDACS is told about it, so misuse is noticed early.

Who else handles it

  • Anthropic (Claude), in the USA: the AI that plans and builds dashboards and answers Ask Relay questions. While it works it receives your descriptions and questions, and samples, summaries and query results of your data.
  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365): sends the sign-in codes and our emails.
  • Cloudflare: the network between your browser and our server. It protects and routes the traffic, so it handles what you send and receive on its way.
  • Razorpay, in India: takes the payment when your organisation buys credits online (card, UPI, netbanking or wallet). It gets your name and email address with the payment, and the payment details you type into its window.
  • Your own warehouse (BigQuery, Snowflake and the like), when you connect one: D2D Relay reads only the tables you choose and never writes to them.

D2D Relay itself runs on PASDACS's own server in India. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your rights, and how to use them

  • See your data: Organisation > Your data > Download my data gives you what D2D Relay holds about you as a file; each dashboard's Power BI file and data can be downloaded from the dashboard.
  • Erase: delete a dashboard (on the dashboard page) or an Ask Relay conversation yourself. Owners can remove people from the organisation. To have your account, or your whole organisation, erased, write to info@pasdacs.com from the address you sign in with.
  • Correct: write to us and we fix it.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you cannot: tell us who, in writing.
  • Complain: write to info@pasdacs.com. We answer within 30 days and settle every grievance within 90 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

How we protect it

  • Connections are encrypted (HTTPS); passwords and keys you give D2D Relay are stored encrypted.
  • Each organisation's data is kept in its own place. The AI works only inside the folder of the dashboard it is building and cannot reach the internet.
  • If a breach ever affects your data, we tell you without delay, with what happened and what you can do.

Children

D2D Relay is a work tool for organisations. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly hold children's data.

Changes

When this notice changes, this page says so with a new date.
